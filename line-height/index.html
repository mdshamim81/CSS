Iron Man

Google me Iron Man is a 2008 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Paramount Pictures, it is the first film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). Directed by Jon Favreau from a screenplay by the writing teams of Mark Fergus & Hawk Ostby and Art Marcum & Matt Holloway, the film stars Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark Iron Man alongside Terrence Howard, Jeff Bridges, Gwyneth Paltrow, Leslie Bibb, and Shaun Toub. Publication History

Premiere

The Creators: He was developed by writer-editor Stan Lee, screenwriter Larry Lieber, artist Don Heck, and legendary character designer Jack Kirby. The First Suit: In his first appearance, the armor wasn't red and gold; it was a bulky, grey suit made of scrap metal. It was painted gold in the very next issue (Tales of Suspense #40) to look less frightening to the public.

Solo series

The Launch (1968): His first independent title, The Invincible Iron Man, debuted in May 1968. This followed a "one-shot" comic called Iron Man and Sub-Mariner that served as a bridge between his old anthology stories and his new solo run.
